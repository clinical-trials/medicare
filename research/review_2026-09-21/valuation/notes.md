Sources: [L-P01 abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39978776/), [L-P02 journal abstract](https://www.auajournals.org/doi/10.1097/UPJ.0000000000000612), [L-P03 full manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8602770/), [NCBI full-text XML](https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pmc&id=8602770).

The studies measure different comparisons. L-P01 uses a broader anatomical match; its accessible abstract does not establish how operative time, global periods or complexity were handled. L-P02 selects comparable surgical approaches and RUC valuation conditions, including total time, global periods, hospital days, postoperative visits and review date. Numerical 95% confidence intervals are unavailable in its abstract despite being specified in Methods; failure to reject a difference is not proof of equivalence. L-P03 normalizes by observed intraoperative time, excluding anesthesia/perioperative time, and weights aggregate results by case volume. Its discussion explicitly acknowledges unmeasured global-period work and exclusion of concurrent procedures. Its 6/12 male-higher RVU pairs and female-higher RVU/hour result must remain visible. These contrasts should not be pooled without a pair-level comparability audit. ([L-P01 Methods](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1089/jwh.2024.0984), [L-P02 Methods](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38758183/), [L-P03 Methods and Discussion](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8602770/))

L-P03's dollar outcomes are **modeled specialty compensation**, calculated using SullivanCotter 2018 specialty dollars/RVU, with historical McGraw-Hill 1994 rates. They are not Medicare conversion factors or observed Medicare claims payments. NSQIP is not a Medicare-only cohort, and patient gender identity and entitlement strata are not established. None of these three studies directly measures patient access, patient out-of-pocket spending or male-versus-female physician payment. Procedure/anatomy labels do not establish a cisgender patient population. Shared pelvic-floor, urinary and mixed/site-dependent oncologic services need their own clinical classifications. ([L-P03 Methods, paragraphs 2–4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8602770/#S6))

Three source issues need explicit handling:

1. **L-P02 pair count:** the journal abstract reports **10 pairs (7 female-higher, 3 male-higher)**; the [authors' AUA News companion](https://auanews.net/issues/articles/2024/july-extra-2024/upj-insight-comparison-of-work-relative-value-units-assigned-to-urological-and-gynecological-surgical-procedures) reports **9 (6 female-higher, 3 male-higher)**. Its table has 19 unique CPT rows. Retain journal counts and flag the discrepancy; do not infer its cause or count the companion as another study.
2. **L-P01 historical subset:** its abstract says **22 CPT codes**, not 22 pairs. The 30% cross-sectional figure and 31–34% historical series also require full-text denominator clarification. The valuation year is 2023; L-P02's exact valuation-data year remains unknown from accessed material.
3. **L-P03 arithmetic:** authors report specialty compensation changing from $44.50 to $52.02/RVU as +14%; the conventional baseline-relative change is **+16.9%**. Table 1 also prints values inconsistent with its stated formula for CPT 58925 and 54135, and an exenteration time difference of 4 minutes despite medians differing by 5. [L-P03_table1.json](L-P03_table1.json) preserves published cells and separates reviewer arithmetic checks. Do not silently replace the source values. IQRs are distribution summaries, not confidence intervals. ([Results and Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8602770/#T1))

PubMed XML and Europe PMC core records matched all three titles, author lists, DOI and PMID identifiers. Neither source returned a linked comment/correction relationship on this check; that is not proof that none exists. Remaining priorities are L-P01/L-P02 lawful full-text access, numerical uncertainty, exact valuation inputs, and pair-level matching/overlap appraisal before quantitative synthesis.
