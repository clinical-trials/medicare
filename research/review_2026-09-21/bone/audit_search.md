# PubMed execution audit — September 21, 2026

This is an independent AI data/parser audit, not independent human screening or PRESS peer review. Scope: `run_pubmed.py`, `prepare_search.py`, archived ESearch responses and request metadata, PMID lists, manifest, summary, sentinel CSV and completed metadata batches. No search query was changed and no final export was run by this auditor.

**Identifier and archive checks passed.** Corrected core **43,904** + supplement **16,866** − overlap **2,084** = **58,686 unique PMIDs**. All **6,769 priority records** are within this union, leaving **51,917** for the remaining metadata export. Priority membership made no eligibility exclusions. Original core and supplemental PMID sets reconstruct exactly from date-partition exports; corrected core adds 60 IDs and removes none. Publication-date partitions are technical retrieval divisions, not review date restrictions. Their overlapping hits are reconciled by PMID.

All 59 archived ESearch responses checked had matching recorded hashes. Request dates run from **2026-09-21 17:13:15 UTC to 17:16:03 UTC**. The original unrecognized `Drug Formularies[Mesh]` warning is preserved; the revised `Formularies as Topic[Mesh]` query has no such warning. Empty-partition messages are retained. No additional ESearch error lists were found. All 6,769 priority XML records match requested IDs and hashes. At the audit snapshot, 8,000 of the remaining records in 20 completed batches also matched their requests and hashes, without overlap with priority; the remainder was still being fetched. This note does not certify the eventual full export.

**Book parser defect found and corrected.** The initial screening export lost titles for 17 PubmedBookArticle records and dates for all 24, because it used journal-article paths. The bounded correction in `prepare_search.py` reads book titles, publication dates, publishers, document type and BookDocument identifiers; it preserves chapter titles and book corporate authors without assigning editors or container authors to chapters. The regression check reproduced the missing title before the patch, then passed all 24 book records. Existing metadata fields were unchanged across 6,745 journal records. [Verification script](test_book_parsing.py) exercises only the real parsing loop in memory, without export side effects. Journal fields remain blank for books; explicit book fields provide the correct container information.

**Known retrieval gap remains.** Sentinel L-G04, Curtis et al. [PMID 18302495](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18302495/), is absent from both searches. Its title/abstract use bone measurement, DXA, screening, testing and “use,” while its MEDLINE headings do not include the core outcome headings. Its searchable text does not match the required core reimbursement/access/utilization vocabulary or the supplementary valuation/cost block. This is an outcome-vocabulary sensitivity gap, not a priority-filter exclusion or technical lost record. Retain its prior-register/citation provenance separately from this run's identification counts. Targeted screening/receipt/uptake/use concepts and clinical-domain supplements warrant formal search review; this audit did not expand the queries.

The ten sentinel studies were selected after execution from the existing register. Retrieval of nine is a diagnostic, not validated recall or a representative sensitivity estimate. Formal PRESS review, additional databases, report-family deduplication and independent human screening remain pending. The root agent separately extended preparation to include remaining metadata only after the complete-export manifest flag exists; the auditor preserved those changes. Final summary counts and identification flags must be regenerated after that export completes.
