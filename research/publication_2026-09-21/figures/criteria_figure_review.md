## Suggested title and caption

**Eligibility framework for the Medicare review in progress**

Caption: The framework distinguishes the investigator’s adopted September 16 population and clinical scope from proposed operational screening rules in the September 21 protocol. It shows evidence routes, not completed selection decisions or PRISMA flow counts. Gender identity is not inferred from recorded sex, anatomy, diagnosis or treatment.

## Concise figure text

### Adopted scope: patient evidence

- **Population:** Cisgender women and men; retain all eligible Medicare ages, including beneficiaries younger than 65.
- **Payer:** Original Medicare, Medicare Advantage or Part D; eligible results must be separately extractable from mixed-payer or mixed-population reports.
- **Care:** Shared body systems/functions; reproductive anatomy or physiology; mixed/site-dependent services classified separately.
- **Question:** Coverage and restrictions; service valuation/payment; patient costs; access, delays, denials or unmet clinical need.

### Proposed operational criteria: empirical evidence

- **Original research** addressing a patient-care or service-valuation question; Medicare basis and relevant results identifiable.
- **Patient evidence:** A relevant sex comparison, need-based assessment within an eligible sex-linked indication/population, or differential sex-related need/access under a common policy.
- **Service evidence:** A defined Medicare service/anatomy valuation question; clinically and resource-appropriate comparisons, or policy consistency when no counterpart exists.
- **Interpretation:** Assess clinical need independently of coverage; retain null and contradictory findings. An opposite-sex comparator is not mandatory.

Output label: **Potentially eligible empirical evidence — final assessment pending**

### Awaiting information: retain as unresolved

- **Male/female recorded; gender identity not reported:** full-text assessment and clarification where feasible; no assumption of confirmed cisgender identity.
- **Essential eligibility or extractable results unclear:** retain pending sufficient information; do not convert nonretrieval into an assessed exclusion.

Output label: **Unresolved eligibility — not automatically excluded**

### Outside the adopted patient/indication scope

- Transgender-focused patient studies.
- Gender-affirming-care indications.
- Combined mixed-population results with no separately extractable eligible cisgender results.

Output label: **Outside primary scope — retain citation and reason in the audit trail**

### Separate evidence streams

- **Policy, formulary and procedure-code sources:** assess Medicare relevance and included indication/service; no individual gender-identity test applies. They cannot establish cisgender patient outcomes.
- **Clinical efficacy/safety and other-payer evidence:** clinical or contextual support; not direct Medicare reimbursement/access effects.
- **Physician sex/gender evidence:** contextual stream; not patient-sex disparity estimates.
- **General health-IT/intervention evidence without the specified equity/need comparison:** translational context; not evidence of closing a sex gap.

Output label: **Retain as labeled context or separate policy/service analysis — do not pool with primary patient estimates**

## Material flags for the diagram

1. **Adopted versus proposed:** The protocol explicitly says it does not supersede `REVIEW_SCOPE.md`. The design list (randomized, quasi-experimental, cohort, case-control, cross-sectional, qualitative), detailed equity-relevance test, database/date/publication limits, and human review procedures are proposed formal methods. Use a legend or panel label rather than presenting all as final adopted criteria. Existing review questions and population boundaries are adopted.
2. **Identity ambiguity is a live decision:** The current scope does not authorize treating sex-coded, identity-unreported cohorts as confirmed cisgender. It also says to retain these as candidates while eligibility is unresolved. Do not draw an automatic “identity not reported → excluded” arrow. A separately labeled expanded analysis would need a dated investigator amendment; none is asserted here.
3. **Mixed cohorts:** A study with separable eligible results can contribute those results. The exclusion applies to the combined, nonseparable estimate, not necessarily the entire publication or every result in it. Other-payer/nonseparable Medicare evidence can remain context.
4. **Policy/code data are not patient cohorts:** Route these before the patient-identity question. Original empirical Medicare code-valuation studies may contribute the separate service synthesis; statutes, fee-schedule descriptions and the project’s own CMS analyses are distinct source/analysis types.
5. **Clinical need is not a coverage gate:** Do not exclude nonrecipients, uninsured services or clinically indicated but noncovered care by defining eligibility from paid claims. Inadequate need adjustment is an appraisal limitation, not automatic exclusion of an otherwise relevant comparison. Differences alone do not prove inequity.
6. **Do not classify all interventions as context:** An intervention addressing an eligible population’s need/access question can qualify for patient evidence (for example, fracture-care outreach for eligible men). General COPD affordability or prescribing-tool studies without the required sex/need equity relevance remain translational context. Clinical benefit does not itself establish Medicare coverage or a reduced sex gap.
7. **No new restrictions:** Do not add age 65+, English-only, free-full-text, cisgender keyword, statistical significance, sexual-orientation, intersex-status, or mandatory male-versus-female comparison requirements. Search sensitivity and final eligibility are different decisions.
8. **Keep counts and completion claims out:** Do not use register counts, search yields, “included studies,” “screened,” “PRISMA-compliant selection,” or completed independent-review language in this figure. Its purpose is to explain the framework while selection is unfinished.
