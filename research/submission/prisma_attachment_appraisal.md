Its footer identifies the source as Page et al., *The PRISMA 2020 statement: an updated guideline for reporting systematic reviews*, **BMJ**. 2021;372:n71 (M-001; PMID 33782057). The checklist contains items 1–27, including subitems 10a–b, 13a–f, 16a–b, 20a–d and 24a–c.

## What this source contributes

This attachment is a reporting aid for the eventual systematic-review report. It requires transparent reporting of the title and abstract, rationale and questions, eligibility rules, information sources and complete search strategies, selection and extraction procedures, outcome and other data items, risk-of-bias methods, effect measures, synthesis and sensitivity methods, missing-results bias, certainty assessment, selection results, study characteristics, individual and synthesis results, discussion, registration/protocol status, support, competing interests, and data/code/material availability.

It does not establish that those procedures were performed. PRISMA is not a quality score, a risk-of-bias instrument, a GRADE rating, a registration, a database-search strategy, or a substitute for independent screening and extraction. PRISMA also does not require that every review perform a meta-analysis; the report must describe and justify the synthesis actually used.

## How it applies to this review

The checklist is now the reporting checklist for the formal Medicare review. The existing [readiness crosswalk](PRISMA_readiness_crosswalk.md) maps each item to the current state and the artifact still needed. The [protocol](Medicare_systematic_review_protocol.md) supplies the prospective operating rules, while PRISMA-S, PRISMA-Equity, design-specific appraisal tools and GRADE/CERQual remain distinct methods sources.

The page numbers in the attachment's “Location where item is reported” column (for example, pages 1, 3, 6–7 and 22–23) are not locations in the Medicare manuscript. They appear to be completed for another report or an example. We therefore retain them as supplied but will replace them with actual manuscript or supplement locations only after the formal search, screening, extraction, appraisal and synthesis are complete.

## Current status against the checklist

The Medicare project is still a prospective methods package plus an exploratory evidence register. It has not yet completed the comprehensive database search, duplicate human screening, full-text eligibility adjudication, formal risk-of-bias assessment, outcome-specific certainty assessment, or reconciled PRISMA flow. The current 34-record inventory and the preliminary white paper cannot populate PRISMA items 16–22 as completed review results. Registration status and the September 16, 2026 scope amendment will be reported truthfully under items 24a–c.

The checklist does not change the population criterion. The active review remains focused on cisgender women and men, with transgender-focused patient studies and gender-affirming indications excluded from the current synthesis; studies that report only male/female coding remain identity-uncertain until full-text assessment. It also does not collapse patient sex/gender evidence, physician sex/gender payment evidence, service/anatomy valuation, and general clinical background into one stream.
