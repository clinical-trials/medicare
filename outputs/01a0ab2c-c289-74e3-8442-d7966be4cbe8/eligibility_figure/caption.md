# Inclusion and exclusion criteria

**Suggested publication caption.** Eligibility framework for the Medicare coverage, payment and access review in progress. The investigator's adopted population and clinical scope (September 16, 2026) is distinguished from proposed operational screening rules in the draft protocol (September 21, 2026). Patient evidence and empirical service-valuation evidence receive separate assessment and synthesis. Reports with unreported gender identity remain unresolved; administrative male/female coding does not establish cisgender identity. Mixed reports can contribute separately extractable eligible findings. Scope exclusions apply to the relevant population, indication or nonseparable estimate rather than automatically to every result in a publication. Code-only findings do not require individual gender-identity assessment; any patient-level component is assessed separately. Policy, clinical, physician-gender and general implementation evidence are retained in labeled contextual streams when useful. These are research-scope decisions, not judgments of study quality or the importance of excluded populations. The figure illustrates criteria, not completed selection decisions or a PRISMA flow diagram.

**Alt text.** A diagram of the Medicare review's eligibility rules. An adopted-scope band identifies cisgender women and men, includes younger Medicare beneficiaries, and distinguishes coverage, payments, patient costs and access. Two inclusion panels cover patient research and empirical service valuation. Red and amber panels distinguish adopted population/indication exclusions from records awaiting information, especially unreported gender identity. A context panel preserves policy, clinical, physician-gender and general implementation evidence. The final panel distinguishes shared systems, reproductive anatomy, reproductive physiology and mixed services, and keeps patient sex separate from physician gender and service anatomy. No study-selection counts are displayed.
